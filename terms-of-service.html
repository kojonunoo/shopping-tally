What the app does

Shopping Tally helps you keep an estimate of your shopping bill. It reads price-label text with your camera, lets you enter or look up items, and keeps a running basket total, budget, checklist and trip history on your device. No account is required.

Totals are estimates

Text recognition, barcode lookups and VAT calculations can be wrong or incomplete. Barcode lookup supplies a product name, not a shelf price. Store prices, offer conditions and tax rounding can differ from what the app shows. Always check recognised prices before adding them, and rely on the store's till receipt as the final amount. Shopping Tally is not financial or tax advice.
