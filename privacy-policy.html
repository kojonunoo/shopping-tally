Shopping Tally Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Shopping Tally is provided by Kojo Nunoo. Contact: emmanuel.nunoo@outlook.com.

Local shopping data

Your basket, shopping list, saved trips, budget and settings are stored on your device. Shopping Tally has no account system or developer-operated cloud sync. Device backup and transfer services can retain app data according to your device settings.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan labels and barcodes. A selected photo is processed for text recognition on-device. The app does not send scan images to the developer or barcode lookup providers. Temporary scan files are removed after processing when possible. The original photo in your gallery is not intentionally modified or deleted.

Barcode lookup

When you choose barcode lookup, the barcode is sent over HTTPS to Open Food Facts and, if no result is found, UPC Item DB to retrieve a product name. The services receive the request and network information such as your IP address and handle it under their own privacy terms. Manual entry and price-label OCR can be used without barcode lookup.

Recognition SDK diagnostics

Text and barcode recognition use Google ML Kit. The OCR model is delivered through Google Play Services and may be downloaded when needed. Recognition is performed on-device after the model is available. Google documents collection of device and app information, installation identifiers, performance metrics and diagnostic events for analytics and diagnostics. This does not mean the SDK makes no network requests.

ML Kit data disclosure · Google privacy policy

Purchases and vouchers

Google Play handles Pro purchases and restores. Shopping Tally receives purchase status and keeps a local entitlement; it does not receive payment-card details. If signed vouchers are enabled, the identifier and voucher are stored locally for verification.

Sharing

If you export a basket, its contents are shared with the app or recipient you choose using Android's share sheet.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it or clear Shopping Tally's app storage in Android settings. Clearing app storage does not delete Google Play purchase records, third-party logs, shared copies or device backups.

Advertising

Shopping Tally does not include advertising SDKs or sell shopping data.
